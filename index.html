Executive Summary

"This is not a social media management contract. This is a structured growth consultancy — where strategy, oversight, and institutional accountability drive real, measurable outcomes for Caregiver Academia Nepal."

Caregiver Academia Nepal operates in a niche that is significantly underrepresented in Nepal's digital content landscape. This proposal presents a 90-day strategic engagement to establish TikTok as a primary awareness and growth channel — while ensuring all content produced is intelligently distributed across Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube for maximum reach.

The engagement is led by Ram Bhandari as the Main Consultant and Digital Growth Strategist. All strategic decisions, creative direction, team supervision, and performance oversight will flow from the Consultant. The internal team at Academia — supervised by Mr. Bikash Chalise — will execute under a clear, Consultant-designed framework. Mr. Hira Tamang will oversee analytics and performance measurement, ensuring data integrity and reporting discipline throughout the engagement.

01 / FOCUS

TikTok View Growth

Primary platform for reach, awareness, and first-contact discovery — driven by algorithmic strategy and consistent content deployment.

02 / FOCUS

Cross-Channel Amplification

03 / FOCUS

Internal Team System

Building a reliable, supervised content production team within Academia — properly briefed, role-assigned, and performance-tracked.

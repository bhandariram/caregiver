Content Strategy & Pillars

All content will be built around 5 strategic pillars, each serving a distinct audience intent and stage of awareness.

🎓

Education & Training Tips

Quick, practical caregiver skills — what to do in real situations. High save rate; positions Academia as the expert source.

❤️

Emotional Stories

Student success journeys, day-in-the-life of a caregiver, impact stories. High share rate; drives brand awareness organically.

💼

Career Opportunity

Scope of caregiving in Nepal and abroad, salary ranges, job placement stories. Drives enrollment inquiries and DMs.

🏥

Behind the Academia

Classroom footage, trainer introductions, facility walkthroughs. Builds trust and reduces hesitation among prospective students.

🌍

Industry & Awareness

Global caregiver demand, elderly care trends in Nepal, healthcare awareness. Establishes thought leadership beyond just promotion.
